🛍️ Novellacart – Shopify Dropshipping Store

A complete eCommerce store setup, product research, marketing, and customer operations

Novellacart is a Shopify-based dropshipping store that I fully managed—from store setup to daily business operations. This project represents my first professional experience in the eCommerce/dropshipping industry, where I handled end-to-end responsibilities, including design, backend setup, marketing, product research, analytics, and customer support.

This repository includes screenshots of the store design, product pages, analytics, and workflow to demonstrate the work I contributed.

🚀 Project Overview

Platform: Shopify
Business Model: Dropshipping
My Role: Store Designer • Product Researcher • Marketing Specialist • Customer Support • Operations Manager

I was responsible for setting up the entire store, optimizing product listings, running paid ads, analyzing performance, and maintaining customer satisfaction through consistent communication and support.

💼 My Key Responsibilities
🌐 1. Shopify Store Setup & Design

Created the entire Shopify store from scratch

Customized theme settings, homepage, collections, menus

Designed high-conversion product pages

Added banners, product images, trust badges, and FAQ section

Integrated payment gateways, shipping profiles, and essential apps

🔍 2. Product Research & Product Addition

Conducted in-depth product research for trending items

Analyzed competition, pricing, and demand

Imported products using DSers / AliExpress

Optimized product titles, descriptions, variants, pricing, tags

Designed product covers and enhanced visuals

📊 3. Marketing & Advertising

Created and managed paid ad campaigns

Designed ad creatives for Facebook, Instagram & TikTok

Analyzed ad performance metrics (CTR, CPC, ROAS)

Identified winning products and scaled their campaigns

Managed brand social media pages

🧾 4. Sales & Store Analytics

Monitored store performance through Shopify analytics

Tracked conversions, traffic sources, abandoned carts

Generated performance reports

Identified weak areas and implemented improvements

👩‍💻 5. Customer Support & Interaction

Responded to customer inquiries on email and social media

Handled order updates, tracking, returns, refunds

Resolved complaints professionally

Maintained positive customer experience and satisfaction

📦 6. Order Fulfillment & Operations

Processed orders via DSers

Updated tracking information

Ensured smooth communication between suppliers and customers

Managed delays, replacements, and after-sale issues
